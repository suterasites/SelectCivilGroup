Turn a Slope into Usable Ground

Bench drops are used to manage level changes on sloped sites, creating flat, usable areas at different heights. Select Civil Group constructs concrete bench drops for residential and commercial properties throughout the Geelong region, providing stable, engineered transitions between different ground levels.

A properly built bench drop prevents erosion, manages water runoff, and creates usable space on land that would otherwise be difficult to develop. We work with your engineer to design and build bench drops that meet structural requirements, integrate with retaining walls and drainage systems, and provide long-term stability for the site.

Andrew is on the tools and on site throughout, so you deal directly with the person running the job from the first quote through to the final level check. No subbie chains, no chasing the office for an update. Most quotes come back within 24 hours of the site walk.

What We Build

From a single level transition to multi-drop solutions on steeply falling blocks, every job is quoted on the specifics of your site.

Level Transitions

Concrete bench drops that step a sloped site down in a controlled way, creating flat, usable areas at each level. Designed to the site's fall and the load they need to carry.

Erosion & Runoff Control

Bench drops that intercept water running down a slope, directing it to drainage rather than letting it carry soil. A key part of protecting a site that has significant fall.

Integrated Retaining

Bench drops designed to work alongside retaining walls where the fall is too great to handle with a single structure. The two systems are built together so they drain and perform as one.

Site Prep & Drainage

Excavation, compaction and drainage installation before the concrete goes in. Getting the subbase right is what makes the finished bench drop hold its shape and drain correctly long term.

Frequently Asked

The questions we get most about concrete bench drops.

A bench drop is a concrete step or series of steps built into a sloped site to create flat, usable areas at different levels. You need one when a slope is too steep to use as-is, when you need to establish a level building platform or usable yard area on a falling block, or when you need to manage water runoff and erosion on sloped ground. They are common on residential blocks, commercial sites and civil works where significant level changes need to be handled in a controlled way.

Cost depends on the number of levels, the height and width of each drop, site access, soil conditions, drainage requirements and whether integration with retaining walls or other structures is needed. Every site is different, and we quote on the specifics rather than giving a blanket rate. We provide an obligation-free written quote within 24 hours once we have the site details.
