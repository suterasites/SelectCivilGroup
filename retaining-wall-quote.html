Walls Built to Hold, Support and Last

Retaining walls are one of the most critical structural elements on any sloped or tiered property. Select Civil Group builds concrete retaining walls designed to hold, support and stabilise soil on residential, commercial and industrial sites across Geelong and the Bellarine Peninsula. Whether you need a single wall to manage a level change in your backyard or a series of engineered walls on a multi-lot development, we deliver work that lasts.

We work with concrete block, poured concrete and reinforced designs depending on the site conditions, engineering requirements and your budget. Every retaining wall we build is constructed to meet relevant Australian standards and local council requirements, with proper drainage, footings and reinforcement included as standard.

Andrew is on the tools and on site, so you deal directly with the person running the job from the first quote through to the final level check. No subbie chains, no chasing the office for an update.

What We Build

From a single garden wall to engineered structural solutions on steep blocks, every wall is quoted on the specifics of your site.

Garden & Landscape Walls

Concrete walls that manage garden levels, terraces and boundary changes. Clean lines, the right finish for your landscape, and drainage built in so water moves where it should.

Structural Retaining Walls

Engineered walls holding back building platforms, driveways and sloping blocks. Built to your engineer's drawings with the footings, steel and backfill the load demands.

Tiered & Multi-Level Walls

Stepped wall solutions for steep falls and large level changes, turning land that is hard to use into flat, usable space that drains and holds.

Drainage & Reinforcement

Ag drains, free-draining backfill and steel reinforcement go into every wall as standard. It is the work you do not see that keeps a wall standing for decades.

How a Job Runs

Quote to handover, the same six steps on every job. No surprises, no scope creep, no chasing for updates.

Step 01

Enquiry

Send the site address, a quick description of the work, and any plans or photos you have. The more we know, the sharper the quote.

Step 02

Site Walk

Andrew visits the block to check access, soil, levels and obstacles the photos do not show. Most quotes come back within 24 hours.

Step 03

Fixed Quote

Itemised, written and locked in. Drainage, spoil cartage and machinery are accounted for so there are no surprises at the end.

Step 04

Programmed Start

Confirmed start and finish dates that we hold to. We coordinate with your builder, engineer or following trades so the ground is ready when they are.

Step 05

On Site

Machine and operator on the ground, working to your engineer's specs. Daily check-ins with whoever is coordinating the build.

Step 06

Handover

Final levels checked, site cleaned, ready for the next stage. Our workmanship is covered for 12 months, and we are a phone call away.

Frequently Asked

The questions we get most about retaining walls.

We build garden and landscape walls, structural walls holding back building platforms and driveways, and tiered multi-level solutions for steep blocks. We work with concrete block, poured concrete and reinforced designs depending on the load, the soil and the site conditions.

Cost depends on the length and height of the wall, the soil being retained, drainage requirements and site access. A short garden wall is a very different job to a structural wall holding a building platform. We provide an obligation-free written quote within 24 hours once we have the site details.

Walls over a certain height, or walls retaining a surcharge like a driveway or building, generally require engineering and may need a permit. We can recommend an engineer we have worked with locally and coordinate the design, or work to your existing engineer's drawings and flag any council requirements before we start.

Most residential retaining walls take three to seven working days from site set-up through to clean-up, depending on length, height and the engineering involved. Larger structural and tiered walls run longer. We confirm a programmed start and finish date as part of the quote.
