What Affects the Cost of a Concrete Job

Every concrete quote is built from the same handful of variables. Knowing what drives cost up or down helps you plan a project that fits the budget without compromising the result.

Volume and area

Cubic metres of concrete and total square metres are the biggest drivers. Bigger pour, higher cost - but per-square-metre rates drop with scale.

Finish

Plain broom is the cheapest. Exposed aggregate, coloured oxide, stencilled and stamped finishes each step up in cost.

Site access

Easy access for trucks and pumps keeps cost down. Tight access, long pump distance or hand-barrow runs add labour.

Reinforcement & engineering

A driveway needs lighter mesh than a raft slab. Heavier engineering specs mean more steel, more depth, more cost.

Site preparation

A flat, prepped block costs less than one needing excavation, fill, compaction or a level platform.

Council & crossovers

New crossovers require council approval and inspection. Always built into the quote where relevant.

Concrete Driveways

A well-built concrete driveway adds value to your property and stands up to years of daily use. Select Civil Group pours and finishes concrete driveways for homes and businesses across the Geelong region, from simple single-car entries to large multi-vehicle driveways and turnaround areas.

We handle the full process - excavation, base preparation, formwork, steel reinforcement, pouring, and finishing. Every driveway is built on properly compacted base material with adequate drainage to prevent cracking and movement over time.

Driveway Finishes Available

  • Plain broom finish - the most common, cleanest look. Lowest cost. Hides minor surface marks.
  • Exposed aggregate - decorative stone exposed through the surface. Premium look, higher slip resistance, higher cost.
  • Coloured concrete - oxide added to the mix. A range of colours to match the property. Mid-range cost.
  • Stencilled or stamped - patterns pressed into the surface before curing. Brick, slate or tile effects. Higher cost.

Concrete Raft Slabs

Raft slabs are the foundation of most new residential and light commercial builds in the Geelong and Bellarine region. Select Civil Group delivers concrete raft slab foundations engineered to suit local soil conditions and building requirements, providing a stable, level base for your construction project.

Our team manages the complete slab process from site preparation and formwork through to reinforcement placement, concrete pouring, and finishing. We work with your builder and engineer to ensure the slab meets all specifications, is poured on schedule, and is finished to the required tolerances. Coordination with plumbers, electricians, and other trades for in-slab services is part of how we operate.

What's Included in a Slab Pour

  • Site set-out from engineer's drawings, formwork to required dimensions
  • Termite barriers, vapour barrier and sand bedding where specified
  • Steel reinforcement (mesh and bars) to AS 2870 and the engineer's design
  • Coordination with plumbers and electricians for in-slab services
  • Concrete pour, screeding, levelling and finish to specified tolerances
  • Curing program and handover to the next trade

Factory Tilt Panels

Tilt-up concrete panels are a fast, cost-effective construction method for factories, warehouses, and commercial buildings. Select Civil Group handles the concrete work for tilt panel construction, including casting beds, panel pouring, and finishing. Concrete Bench Drops

Bench drops are used to manage level changes on sloped sites, creating flat, usable areas at different heights. Select Civil Group constructs concrete bench drops for residential and commercial properties throughout the Geelong region, providing stable, engineered transitions between different ground levels.

A properly built bench drop prevents erosion, manages water runoff, and creates usable space on land that would otherwise be difficult to develop. We work with your engineer to design and build bench drops that meet structural requirements, integrate with retaining walls and drainage systems, and provide long-term stability for the site.
