Ground Prepared Right, Across Greater Geelong

Geelong keeps building. The southern corridor through Armstrong Creek, Charlemont and Mount Duneed is still turning former farmland into streets, Lara and the northern suburbs keep growing, and infill projects keep appearing across the established parts of the city. Every one of those jobs starts with earthworks, and whatever happens at that stage carries through to each trade that follows.

The ground changes from one part of Geelong to the next. Reactive clay is common, basalt runs under a lot of the western suburbs, and clay sites get heavy and slow to work after rain. We plan the machinery, the compaction and the program around what is actually in the ground, working to your engineer's specification with laser-guided grading so the finished levels land where the drawings say.

Spoil cartage and legal disposal are part of our scope, so there is no separate truck contractor to chase, and larger volumes can be handled through our excess soil removal service. Andrew is on site for the work, so the person you talk to about your levels is the person setting them.

How an Earthworks Job Runs in Geelong

From the first set of drawings to handing over a buildable site, the order of work stays the same.

Step 01

Plans and Scope

Send the address with any engineering drawings or subdivision plans. For a simple lot, photos and a description are enough to start.

Step 02

Site Walk

We look at access, existing levels, vegetation and where material can be stockpiled or loaded out.

Step 03

Itemised Quote

Clearing, cut and fill, compaction and cartage are each priced as their own line, in writing.

Step 04

Clear and Strip

Vegetation comes out and the topsoil is stripped, leaving a working surface ready for the machines.

Step 05

Cut, Fill and Compact

Material is moved to the engineer's levels and fill is compacted to spec, with testing booked where it is needed.

Step 06

Handover

Final levels checked, excess spoil carted away, and the ground handed to the next trade ready to build on.
