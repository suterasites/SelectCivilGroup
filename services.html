Concrete Services

Driveways, retaining walls, raft slabs, paving, tilt panels, and custom concrete work for residential, commercial, and industrial projects.

Retaining Walls

Retaining walls are one of the most critical structural elements on any sloped or tiered property. Select Civil Group builds concrete retaining walls designed to hold, support, and stabilise soil on residential, commercial, and industrial sites across Geelong and the Bellarine Peninsula. Whether you need a single wall to manage a level change in your backyard or a series of engineered walls on a multi-lot development, we deliver work that lasts.

We work with concrete block, poured concrete, and reinforced designs depending on the site conditions, engineering requirements, and your budget. Every retaining wall we build is constructed to meet relevant Australian standards and local council requirements, with proper drainage, footings, and reinforcement included as standard.

Concrete Driveways

A well-built concrete driveway adds value to your property and stands up to years of daily use. Select Civil Group pours and finishes concrete driveways for homes and businesses across the Geelong region, from simple single-car entries to large multi-vehicle driveways and turnaround areas.

We handle the full process - excavation, base preparation, formwork, steel reinforcement, pouring, and finishing. We offer plain, exposed aggregate, and coloured concrete finishes to match your property and preferences. Every driveway is built on properly compacted base material with adequate drainage to prevent cracking and movement over time.

Concrete Raft Slabs

Raft slabs are the foundation of most new residential and light commercial builds in the Geelong and Bellarine region. Select Civil Group delivers concrete raft slab foundations engineered to suit local soil conditions and building requirements, providing a stable, level base for your construction project.

Our team manages the complete slab process from site preparation and formwork through to reinforcement placement, concrete pouring, and finishing. We work with your builder and engineer to ensure the slab meets all specifications, is poured on schedule, and is finished to the required tolerances. Coordination with plumbers, electricians, and other trades for in-slab services is part of how we operate.

Concrete Paving

Concrete paving is a durable, low-maintenance solution for paths, patios, courtyards, pool surrounds, and outdoor entertaining areas. Select Civil Group delivers concrete paving work across Geelong and the Bellarine Peninsula for both residential and commercial properties, creating clean, functional surfaces that hold up to the local climate and heavy use.

We work with a range of finishes including broom finish, exposed aggregate, stamped patterns, and coloured concrete to suit your design preferences and budget. Every paved area is built on a properly prepared base with appropriate falls for drainage, expansion joints to manage movement, and reinforcement where required.

Factory Tilt Panels

Tilt-up concrete panels are a fast, cost-effective construction method for factories, warehouses, and commercial buildings. Select Civil Group handles the concrete work for tilt panel construction, including casting beds, panel pouring, and finishing. We work alongside builders and project managers to deliver panels on schedule and to specification.

Our team understands the precision required for tilt panel work. Panels need to be uniform in thickness, properly reinforced, and finished to tight tolerances so they fit together correctly when lifted into position. We bring the equipment, the materials, and the attention to detail needed to get tilt panel concrete right every time.

Concrete Seating

Concrete seating provides a permanent, weather-resistant solution for public spaces, parks, commercial properties, and residential outdoor areas. Select Civil Group builds custom concrete seating across Geelong and the Bellarine, from simple bench forms to integrated landscape seating walls and tiered designs.

Built-in concrete seating is low maintenance, vandal-resistant, and can be finished in a variety of textures and colours to match your landscape design. We work with architects, landscape designers, and councils to deliver seating that meets both functional and aesthetic requirements, built to endure years of daily use and the local coastal climate.

Concrete Bench Drops

Bench drops are used to manage level changes on sloped sites, creating flat, usable areas at different heights. Select Civil Group constructs concrete bench drops for residential and commercial properties throughout the Geelong region, providing stable, engineered transitions between different ground levels.

A properly built bench drop prevents erosion, manages water runoff, and creates usable space on land that would otherwise be difficult to develop. We work with your engineer to design and build bench drops that meet structural requirements, integrate with retaining walls and drainage systems, and provide long-term stability for the site.

Earthworks & Excavation

Site preparation, land clearing, precision excavation, and environmental services to get your project started on solid ground.

Earthworks

Every construction project starts with the ground. Select Civil Group provides earthworks services across the Geelong and Bellarine region, including land clearing, grading, levelling, bulk cut and fill, and soil compaction. Whether you are preparing a single residential lot or a multi-stage subdivision, we have the machinery and the experience to get the ground right.

Proper earthworks are the foundation of everything that follows. Poorly prepared ground leads to drainage problems, foundation issues, and costly rework down the line. We use laser-guided grading equipment and work to engineering specifications to ensure your site is prepared correctly the first time, with spoil removal and disposal handled as part of the service.

Site Cuts

Site cuts create the level building platform needed before any construction can begin. Select Civil Group performs precision site cuts for new homes, extensions, sheds, and commercial buildings throughout Geelong and the surrounding region. We cut to the exact levels specified by your engineer, ensuring the site is ready for footings and slabs without delays.

Our operators work with modern excavation equipment and laser levels to achieve accurate, consistent results. We handle the full scope of work including topsoil stripping, cut and fill balancing, compaction, and spoil removal. If your block has a slope, a site cut is the first step to turning it into a buildable surface, and getting it right at this stage saves time and money on everything that follows.

Detail Excavation

Detail excavation is the precise digging work required for footings, trenches, service trenches, stormwater lines, and other below-ground structures. Select Civil Group performs detail excavation on residential and commercial sites across Geelong, working to engineering drawings and specifications to deliver accurate, clean results.

We use a range of excavators suited to tight-access sites and confined spaces, ensuring we can get the job done efficiently without damaging surrounding structures, landscaping, or services. Detail excavation demands accuracy, and our operators have the experience to dig to exact depths, widths, and profiles specified by your engineer or builder.

Removal of Excess Oil

Excess oil on construction and industrial sites can create environmental hazards, compromise soil integrity, and cause compliance issues. Select Civil Group provides professional removal of excess oil from sites across the Geelong and Bellarine region, ensuring your site meets environmental and safety standards before the next phase of work begins.

We handle the assessment, removal, and responsible disposal of excess oil and contaminated materials in accordance with EPA Victoria guidelines. Whether the oil is from machinery spillage, underground tank leaks, or previous industrial use, we work efficiently to remediate the area so your project can proceed without delays or regulatory complications.

Our Process

How a Job Runs, Quote to Handover

We run the same playbook on every job, whether it is a single driveway or a multi-stage commercial site. Six clear stages, no surprises, and a single point of contact from the first call through to final handover.

  1. 01 Site Visit and Quote

    We come out, we scope it, you get a written quote

    Andrew walks the site with you, takes measurements, photographs the existing conditions, and flags anything that will affect the price (access, soil, services). A written, fixed-price quote lands in your inbox within 48 hours of the visit. No call centre, no quote chasing.

  2. 02 Scope and Sign-Off

    Plans reviewed, permits flagged, scope locked

    We work through your plans or engineer drawings, confirm the right materials and finish, and flag any council, permit or service-locate work that has to be lined up before we mobilise. You sign off on a clear scope so everyone is working from the same page on day one.

  3. 03 Schedule and Mobilise

    Locked-in start date, crew and plant booked

    You get a firm start date the moment you sign off. We line up the plant (excavator, posi-track, tipper), order materials to land the morning we start, and confirm site access. If your timeline shifts, we will tell you within 24 hours whether we can move with it.

  4. 04 On Site

    Daily progress, real photos, decisions handled fast

    Andrew runs the job from the cab. You get progress photos at the end of each day, a quick call if anything changes on site, and one direct number for any question that comes up. No subbie chain, no chasing the office.

  5. 05 Finish and Handover

    Site cleaned, spoil removed, ready for the next trade

    We do not call a job finished until the site is clean, the spoil is off, and the surface is ready for the next trade or final inspection. You get a final walk-through with Andrew, photographs of the completed work, and a tax invoice the same week.

  6. 06 Aftercare

    12-month workmanship cover, callbacks handled fast

    Our workmanship is covered for 12 months after handover. If anything settles, cracks or moves outside spec in that window, Andrew is back on site to make it right. We are a local Geelong outfit, not a fly-in crew, so callbacks are a phone call away.

Most residential jobs run from quote to handover in two to four weeks. Commercial and multi-stage projects scale from there. Either way, you know what is happening at every stage.
